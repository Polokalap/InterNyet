This website is entirely [donation funded](/donation), and the moderation is kept to the minimum. We do not use human moderators, as humans tend to be biased on controversial topic, and politics. We believe there should be a place where users can post and discuss their ideas freely, anonymously.
That said, we do have rules to keep conversations productive, and avoid [shitposting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shitposting).

## 1: Post quality
We ask you to make your posts readable for an english audience. You can use foreign words for context, but the main thread should be in english.
Please follow the topic of the board. If you are not sure where your post belongs, post it in [/r/](/board/r). If it remotely releates to the topic of the board, you can post it.
When posting something controversial, or [radical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical) or unpopular, please explain your position if you feel like you need to, or your position isn't clear to an average Joe.

## 2: Graphic media & CSAM
[Child Sexual Abuse Material](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_pornography) is not allowed on this website in any way, shape or form. This is the only thing I scout for and remove manually.
Posting gore, or any graphic media is not allowed. Please do not post media like that. These rules also apply to your profile picture.

## 3: Your, and others' privacy
Let us be clear first. We collect a [fingerprint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fingerprint_(computing)) based on the data your browser shares. We store it salted, and hashed in our database to avoid trolls using the site after getting blocked or rate limited from the site. We use your IP address as well, but let me be very clear about this: **We do not sell, or distribute this data in any way to anyone.** We only use it to avoid spamming. Please understand that. We have no interest in your private life, what websites you visit. If you do not trust us, which you shouldn't, we recommend [Mullvad Browser](https://mullvad.net/en/browser), or [Brave](https://brave.com), as these browsers have protection against fingerprinting. I personally recommend [NymVPN](https://nym.com), as I've been using it for a nice amount of time now, and it's been reliable, and build really well. We are not affiliated in any way with these services.
Now let's talk about what we expect from you. Sharing other user's personal information without permission is strictly prohibited. Treat others the way you'd like to be treated.

## 4: Pornography
You are allowed to post pornography on the website. Please keep it to the minimum tho, and on keep it on topic with the board. I personally do not want this site to become the next [4chan](https://4chan.org), so please try to keep it to the minimum. Child Sexual Abuse Material is not allowed, as discussed in section 2.

## 5: Profile picture, display name
Using Child Sexual Abuse Material, graphic violence or anything related to harming yourself or others in your profile picture or display name is prohibited. It'll result in the removal of your profile picture, or possibly in banning your account.
